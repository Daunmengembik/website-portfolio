Overview

During my internship at BRIN I worked on refurbishing water quality monitoring devices. The job covered the whole device: finding out why it misbehaved, fixing the hardware, correcting the sensor readings, then improving the firmware and the screen that people read the data from.

Diagnosis, repair and recalibration

I worked through each device in the same order:

  • Diagnosed the issues in each device.
  • Recalibrated the sensors so their readings could be trusted again.
  • Repaired or replaced faulty components where necessary.

Firmware

I rewrote the Arduino C++ firmware to improve the system's reliability and functionality.

Display interface

I redesigned the interface on the Nextion display so that real-time data is easier to read and the device is easier to use.
