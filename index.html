About

An electrical and embedded systems engineer from Indonesia. I do and love designing and integrating hardware for embedded, sensor-driven systems and system architecture including: sensor nodes, firmware, wireless links, and the schematics that tie them together.

At Inovast Consulting I design a distributed field monitoring system with Raspberry Pi and ESP32, connected by an ESP-NOW mesh. Design schematics and system architecture in Altium Designer and EasyEDA, select and integrate sensors (pulse oximeter, gas, IMU, GPS) into distributed IoT nodes, and validate hardware through I2C debugging, sensor calibration, and field testing. also optimized power and communication payloads for resource-constrained hardware, and collaborate directly with firmware engineers to bring designs from schematic to working prototype.

hold a Degree in Electronics Engineering from Bandung State Polytechnic. Sufficient in C/C++ and Python for firmware and draw schematics in Altium Designer and EasyEDA.
